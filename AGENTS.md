# FOGBOUND project conventions

## Art direction

Keep the whole game in one coherent visual style: finely painted overhead ship miniatures on a continuous deep petroleum-blue sea chart, with weathered teak, desaturated steel, ivory highlights and aged brass. Reuse the existing fleet, sea and lighting as references for every new asset. Ship sprites use transparent backgrounds and fit their complete logical footprint without stretching.

Do not mix flat schematic ship silhouettes, cartoon or pixel-art sprites into the painted fleet. Research professional asset sites when needed and record reference sources in the art documentation. Treat outside material as reference, and use original or appropriately licensed production assets. Preserve the established board, frame, effects and typography when making bounded art changes.

## Turns

The placement phase has a 90-second limit. Battle turns have no time limit and advance only when the acting player explicitly ends the turn, including at zero action points. Practice AI ends its own turn using the same pass action. Fighter aircraft may launch under enemy air control and capture it for three of the new owner's turns.
